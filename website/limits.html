The limits, printed in full

What Archx does not do

  • Prevent screenshots. OS-level and extension captures happen above page JavaScript. Archx may degrade them (interlace, readback poisoning), but does not veto them. The 99-case fixed image-transform bench recovered StegoMark in 85 cases and does not include screenshot or camera captures; separate browser tests check viewer capture defenses, not mark recovery from captures. Misses remain possible.
  • Stop a camera. Temporal interlace and moiré-unfriendly patterns reduce quality; physics wins eventually. Attribution is the control here.
  • Survive AI regeneration. Published diffusion attacks reduce or remove many tested invisible watermark schemes, including neural methods. Archx’s StegoMark has not been measured against full diffusion regeneration; treat it as vulnerable rather than assuming it survives. The periodic-text synthetic stress host recovered 0/11, which does not predict arbitrary synthetic or AI-generated assets. Test representative catalog content and keep issuance records. Related reference: arXiv:2602.22197 (February 2026; IEEE SaTML 2026). If your threat is “regenerate my catalog”, you need provenance + legal process, not only marks.
  • Prove collusion alone. The assay ranks suspects for investigation; Tardos’ bound puts blind collusion-proof decoding outside a 96-bit channel. Anyone claiming otherwise at this payload size is selling something.
  • Replace access control. If everyone may view, everyone may copy at display quality. Archx assumes an authorize decision exists.

Known measured edges

  • Adversarial periodic/synthetic content: 0/11 robust recovery in the bench (kept in the corpus deliberately).
  • 1-bit re-binarization destroys the mark (a measured dead end for survival at acceptable quality).
  • Layout-4 marks under 0.7-scale photo crops can exhaust the shipped bench's 20 s auto-detection budget (pin the layout or raise the budget — pinned searches recover them).
  • Robust-profile distortion is ≈28.6 dB PSNR class: visible to pixel-peepers, chosen knowingly (D-12).
  • maxWidthFraction defaults to 1; an entitled viewer may receive native-width pixels when other caps permit. Set it below 1 for preview-only delivery; if the fraction would cap below 64 px, delivery is denied rather than rounded up.
  • Transplanted marked regions carry their pixels’ mark with them — attribution must be corroborated with issuance records, as the guide describes.
