The limits, printed in full

What Archx does not do

  • Prevent screenshots. OS-level and extension captures happen above page JavaScript. Archx degrades them (interlace, readback poisoning) and attributes them (StegoMark survives screenshot chains in the bench) — it does not veto them.
  • Stop a camera. Temporal interlace and moiré-unfriendly patterns reduce quality; physics wins eventually. Attribution is the control here.
  • Survive AI regeneration. Diffusion re-synthesis drops every tested invisible watermark (ours and the neural ones) to near-zero decode in the literature. If your threat is “regenerate my catalog”, you need provenance + legal process, not only marks.
  • Prove collusion alone. The assay ranks suspects for investigation; Tardos’ bound puts blind collusion-proof decoding outside a 96-bit channel. Anyone claiming otherwise at this payload size is selling something.
  • Replace access control. If everyone may view, everyone may copy at display quality. Archx assumes an authorize decision exists.

Known measured edges

  • Adversarial periodic/synthetic content: 0/11 robust recovery in the bench (kept in the corpus deliberately).
  • 1-bit re-binarization destroys the mark (a measured dead end for survival at acceptable quality).
  • Layout-4 marks under 0.7-scale photo crops can exhaust a 20 s auto-detection budget (pin the layout or raise the budget — pinned searches recover them).
  • Robust-profile distortion is ≈28.6 dB PSNR class: visible to pixel-peepers, chosen knowingly (D-12).
  • Transplanted marked regions carry their pixels’ mark with them — attribution must be corroborated with issuance records, as the guide describes.
