Modes & profiles, measured

Three one-line starting points — every individual flag stays adjustable afterwards. Switch profiles and watch what each one turns on, and what it costs. Numbers are from npm run bench:opt on a 2 vCPU box; your hardware will differ, the ratios are the point.

Cost per image

Server work is dominated by the photo encode; token minting is ~0.25 ms each in batches. Warm delivery after the first view is served from bounded caches.

    04

    When a copy surfaces

    You find your picture on a forum. Rescan it offline — no server, no phone-home. The detector reads the invisible per-recipient fingerprint back out of the pixels and tells you which copy it was. What it can and cannot survive is measured, not asserted:

    $ archx-detect suspect.jpg --key-env ARCHX_STEGO_KEY

    {
  "found": true,
  "fingerprint": 9182736450,
  "recipient": "buyer-1042@example.com",
  "layout": 3,
  "note": "compare against your issuance log before accusing anyone"
}

    The fingerprint is evidence for your investigation — corroborate with issuance logs and a perceptual-hash match before acting on it.
